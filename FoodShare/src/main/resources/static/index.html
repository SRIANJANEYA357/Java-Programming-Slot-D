How FoodShare Works

A simple, transparent 4-step workflow to fight hunger and cut waste

📢

1. List Surplus

Banquets, restaurants and individuals post available food with quantity, unit and expiry hours.

🔍

2. Discover & Claim

Shelters and recipients claim items instantly with concurrency-safe quantity checks and priority handling.

🎟️

3. Secure Token

The system creates a one-time cryptographic collection token ensuring zero unauthorized pickups.

🚚

4. Verify & Distribute

Providers verify the token on pickup, or volunteers deliver to offline camps with full audit trails.
