Our Mission

Zero Food Waste. Zero Hunger.

FoodShare empowers communities, caterers, banquet organizers and restaurants to redistribute high-quality surplus food transparently.

The Problem We Are Solving

In urban cities, millions of tons of fresh, nutritious food are discarded after weddings, corporate conferences and restaurant services every evening, while nearby orphanages, old age shelters and vulnerable families struggle for nourishment.

FoodShare bridges this logistical and transparency gap through an intelligent web platform that matches surplus providers with verified recipients and community distribution volunteers in real time.

🌱 Core Objectives

  • Reduction of Food Wastage: Enable instant posting of banquet and restaurant surplus.
  • Equitable Distribution: Priority allocation algorithm giving preference to children shelters and senior care homes.
  • Cryptographic Verification: One-time collection tokens eliminating fraudulent claims and duplicate pickups.
  • Volunteer Empowerment: Facilitating offline food distribution camps and direct vehicle dispatch.

🛡️ System Architecture

  • Java & Spring Boot 3.3: Robust RESTful microservices with transactional integrity.
  • Database Row-Level Locking: Pessimistic locking guaranteeing zero overbooking under high traffic.
  • MySQL Relational Store: Comprehensive relational audit logs for all distributed meals.
  • Role-Based Security: BCrypt encryption and JWT session tokens.
